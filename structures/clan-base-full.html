clan_base_full

The clan's shared base, still standing whole — the old players' home.

The complete, undamaged version of the old clan base, this was the shared home of the server's old players before the corruption reached it. Finding this variant means the collapse had not started here yet.

Somewhere inside is the Evidence Chest, holding the Evidence CD, Elias Morrow's last known recording.

Lore

The full origin story, recovered chapter by chapter — from Elias Morrow's first experiments to Rezxqu's final sacrifice.

Chapter I

Elias, the Developer Who Went Too Far

A developer working behind the scenes at Mojang, Elias Morrow wanted to bring unusual ideas into Minecraft: glitch creatures, distorted dimensions, living code that reacts to the player's behavior. The studio warned him: "This isn't a game anymore. This is something else."

When his ideas were rejected, Elias became obsessed. He secretly began writing a mod... he called it The Corrupted Code. This mod contained no content of its own. Instead, it rewrote Minecraft's own files, creating the first codeworm-like entity.

Chapter II

The Birth of Integrion

Elias began building his own thoughts, fears, and anger into the mod:

  • his identity → Integrion
  • his fears → Brokenone
  • his paranoia → Skinwalker

The mod was, in essence, the shattered pieces of Elias's mind. One day Elias released the mod on a survival server under the name of "testing." The server was never the same again.

Chapter III

The Dark Altar Is Built

The mod began manipulating one of the players: an ordinary guy, XxDYE_Xx. His chat slowly changed. Code started appearing.

"build…"
"dig deeper…"
"deeper still…"

One day, it was finally finished: the Altar, which shattered the Bedrock. When it was activated, the depths cracked open, and three beings emerged from the Void:

  • Skinwalker — who has no form of his own
  • Brokenone — a thirteen-piece colossus freed from the Moon
  • Integrion — Elias's distorted personality, pure code and hatred

The server collapsed within minutes. Players either died and became entities, or vanished from the logs, as if they had never existed.

Chapter IV

The Final Survivor: Rezxqu

Only one player remained alive: Rezxqu. He watched as Skinwalker slipped into other players, Brokenone tore itself free from the Moon, Integrion began speaking in chat in a distorted, radio-like voice, and the world corrupted, piece by piece.

Rezxqu knew the only way to save the world was to seal them away. So he performed three rituals.

1. Sealing the Skinwalker
Beneath the server's ancient mines lay a space where the Skinwalker's "heart" was kept. Rezxqu found it, and with a block-breaking ritual, sealed the Skinwalker into the depths forever. Since then, in the mines, you can hear: "I will come back…"

2. Sealing Brokenone in the Moon
The Moon cracked every night. Rezxqu realized that the Moon was Brokenone's "home." With a high-power ritual, he tore open the Void and pulled Brokenone back into the Moon, where it fused again, shattered into 13 pieces.

3. The Final Sacrifice — Sealing Integrion
Rezxqu knew Integrion was different. It was Elias's mind itself. It was living code itself. It could not be killed. The only way to seal it was for him to fall into the Void himself. The ritual sacrificed him, and dragged Integrion down with it.

Rezxqu's last chat message:

"if anyone ever comes back here… I beg you… don't finish what we started… no one remembers us, this world is our grave, and the server is our cathedral..."

The server never appeared on the list again.

Chapter V

The Origin World

The player can enter this world, where the events once took place. Lifeless. Silent. Corrupted. Many buildings still stand there — the old homes of players who once lived on the server.

They can find a book in a chest: XxDYE_Xx's Diary. And later, more, hidden.

Hidden sections also exist, containing books that tell the lore. Part of a book's contents:

"deep beneath the void… don't go to it."

And later another one:

"everyone comes up from beneath the bedrock… but you must go to Integrion…"

The Player's Story

When the player enters with the mod:

  • everything is distorted
  • the chat reads: Nothing_is_alive joined the game

It watches, helps… or warns.

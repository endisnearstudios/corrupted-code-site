music_disc_evidence

Plays evidence.ogg in a jukebox, with glitch particles while it spins.

Music Disc "Evidence" is the most recently added RecordItem. Placed in a jukebox, it plays evidence.ogg, and glitch particles appear above the jukebox for as long as it is playing, watched server-side by the EvidenceRecordManager.

Its comparator output is 15, matching the rarest vanilla discs, and its length is set to roughly 195 seconds to match the playback and comparator timing. It carries RARE item rarity, and a stack size of 1.

Found inside the Evidence Chest at Clan Base, it is the physical form of the Evidence CD, Elias Morrow's last known recording, and the first direct evidence that Integrion made contact with him during the project's development.

Void Rooms

Known internally as the "Integrated Void."

Known internally as the "Integrated Void," Void Rooms is a block labyrinth the player must navigate to find the control block. Entering the correct code, 2013, into it is the only way out.

The structure also plays a role in the Level 3 endgame: it appears at midnight on Day 6, freezing time until the control block is solved.

Dead Void

An infinite, corrupted bedrock plane.

An infinite, flat plane of corrupted bedrock, and the mod's final destination. The Integrion altar stands here, placing the Integrion Core on it is what summons the final bossfight.

The Dead Void is only reached by falling through the dead void gate that opens at the center of the world on Day 7 of the Level 3 countdown.

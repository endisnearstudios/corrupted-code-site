Purgatory

purgatory ... purgatory_layer13

Purgatory is the mod's largest, chained dimension system, thirteen separate Minecraft dimensions linked together, each with its own generator and its own NBT-based "tiling," assembling chunks out of prebuilt structure pieces the same way the Integrated Void does.

Every layer can be haunted by Blackhead, Blackhead2, Blackhead3, Eye Player or Purgatory Humanoid, using the same mechanics described on their own entity pages. On death and on every dimension change, the mod deliberately clears any "chaser" state tied to the player so nothing gets stuck incorrectly.

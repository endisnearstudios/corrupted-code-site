13 Layers Dimensions

Purgatory

purgatory ... purgatory_layer13

Thirteen stacked limbo layers make up Purgatory, the dimension the player descends through via scripted falls and portals. Each layer carries its own structures and inhabitants, nothing about the descent is randomized.

Layers 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 are wandered by the Purgatory Humanoid, which wears a different skin on each. Layer 4 has its own dedicated Someone Is Watching variant found nowhere else in the dimension.

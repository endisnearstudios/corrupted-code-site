Commands

Player and admin commands shipped with the mod.

CommandDescription
/question [target]Sets or queries which "speaker" — nothing_is_alive or null — responds to chat questions.
/unsafemodeEnables real desktop interactions (camera, notepad, cmd), gated behind a confirmation screen.
/safemodeSwitches back to safe mode.
/fastevents [2|4]Usable by anyone; speeds up the ambient event/spawn rate to 2x or 4x.
/sloweventsUsable by anyone; resets the event/spawn rate back to 1x.

Integrion

The mod's central antagonist.

200
HP

The mod's central antagonist, an AI that consumed its own creator, described in the lore only as a "watching form." Its behaviour is controlled by the IntegrionManager, and it remains dormant until the Level 3 endgame ritual is completed.

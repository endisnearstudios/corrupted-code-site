Moon Skinwalker

Appears during the Distorted Moon event in place of a Player Imposter.

A Skinwalker variant tied to the Distorted Moon event, the three-night cycle exclusive to Corruption Level 1, appearing in place of a Player Imposter.

Throwing it into the void also advances World Corruption from Level 1 to Level 2, the same as the base Skinwalker.

Skinwalker

The Level 1 bossfight and the mod's signature stalker.

80
HP
0.50

10
Dmg

The base Skinwalker is the mod's first real threat, a GeckoLib-animated stalker built around a simple, unsettling question: who you can trust in the dark. Its behaviour and animation states are controlled by the SkinwalkerManager.

Skinwalker is also the Level 1 bossfight. Throwing a defeated Skinwalker into the void is what advances World Corruption to Level 2.

The Broken One

The Level 2 bossfight, tied to the Moon events.

9999
HP
200
Dmg

The top-tier threat of the Broken Moon event, and the Level 2 bossfight. Its damage output alone makes unprepared encounters effectively unsurvivable.

Throwing a defeated Broken One into the void is what advances World Corruption to Level 3, the final countdown.

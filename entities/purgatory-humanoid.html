Purgatory Humanoid

The wandering resident of Purgatory's mid layers.

20
HP
2.20
Speed

Purgatory Humanoid wanders layers 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 of the Purgatory dimension, wearing a different skin on each layer it inhabits. Unlike most entities in the mod, it is not blocked by walls, it simply passes through them.

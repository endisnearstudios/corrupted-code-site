Mineshaft Skinwalker (False)

A deceptive fake version found in the same mineshafts.

Found in the same mineshafts as its counterpart, the "False" variant is built to deceive rather than chase directly. It disappears the moment the player gets close, and a fake creeper spawns in its place, with the real Skinwalker waiting inside.

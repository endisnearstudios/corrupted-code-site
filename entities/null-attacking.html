Null Attacking

An aggressive null manifestation.

Once triggered through chat, Null Welcome's passive greeting gives way to Null Attacking, the aggressive form fired off by the mod's internal chat-attack sequence.

It chases and deals damage like a corrupted mob rather than a scripted event, and its appearance usually coincides with a nearby spike in ambient glitch activity.

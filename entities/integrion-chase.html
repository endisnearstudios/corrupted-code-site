Integrion (Chase Variant)

Summoned by chat, this Integrion actually gives chase.

Typing "rot in hell integrion" into chat summons a second, more direct form of Integrion after a 4-second delay, one that actively chases rather than watching from a distance.

Getting caught is an instant kill, followed by a kick with the message "the end is high."

Boiled One

Freezes the player, then reveals their own clone.

2048
HP
8
Dmg

Boiled One's automatic daily trigger is disabled; it now only appears through the /ccode command or the Corruption Level 2 random spawn pool.

On spawning, the player freezes in place for 10 seconds with a nausea effect, after which their own clone, a Player Imposter, appears as a screenshot-style jumpscare.

The aggressive "day 14" variant now automatically disappears after 18 seconds if not defeated, and its screen overlay switches off after 20 seconds at the latest.

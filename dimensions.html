Dimensions

Five worlds, five stages of corruption. Each dimension listed here has its own section and inhabitants.

13 layers · purgatory … purgatory_layer13

Purgatory

Thirteen stacked limbo layers. The player descends through them via scripted falls and portals — each layer has its own structures and inhabitants. Layers 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 are wandered by the Purgatory Humanoid (a different skin per layer); layer 4 has its own dedicated Someone Is Watching variant. The purgatory_next block family provides portals to the following layer.

Endgame

Dead Void

An infinite, flat, corrupted bedrock plane. The Integrion altar stands here — placing the Integrion Core on it summons the final Integrion bossfight. Reached by falling through the Day 7 dead void gate at the center of the world.

Puzzle

Void Rooms

The "Integrated Void" block labyrinth. The player must locate the control block and enter a code (2013) into it to exit the dimension and unfreeze time in the endgame sequence.

Mirror dimension

Ghost World

A complete, depopulated mirror of the overworld, wandered by Ghost Humanoids — the spirits of old, dead players.

Encounter dimension

Dark Void

The dark void dimension tied to the Lost Player encounter.

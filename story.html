World Corruption 0–3

Story

The tale of a vanished studio, told in fragments — chat messages, subtitles and jump scares — until the player faces Integrion in the depths of the Dead Void.

Overview

A save file that becomes aware

The mod's core premise: your own Minecraft save file "wakes up" and gradually corrupts. This is called WORLD CORRUPTION in-mod, with three escalating levels — each unlocking new creatures and events, and each making the world's appearance more distorted: darkened moon textures, sinking chunks, error blocks bleeding into the terrain.

The overall arc: an AI that consumed its developer, then corrupted the test server and everyone who played on it.

Timeline

World Corruption — levels 1 to 3

Level 1

The First Cracks

Rare events begin: the Watcher, Lost Player, Siluet, Whos5, Null and Nothing Is Alive start appearing — "someone is watching." Everything still seems explainable.

  • Event: Distorted Moon (3-night event) — distorted animals begin spawning.
  • Bossfight: Skinwalker. Throwing the Skinwalker into the void advances World Corruption.
Level 2

Taking Hold

New entities surface: Boiled One, the Blackhead variants in Purgatory, Eye Player, Someone Is Watching, Lost Admin, the Wendigo, and The Broken One.

  • Event: Broken Moon (16-night event).
  • Bossfight: The Broken One. Throwing The Broken One into the void advances World Corruption.
Level 3

The Collapse

The overworld's time resets to the morning of "Day 0," and a permanent, distorted moon texture takes hold. A seven-day countdown begins.

  • Day 4: Elias Morrow's ghost (Elias_Th3_D3v) appears in chat with a Caesar-cipher monologue.
  • Every day: a random nearby chunk visually collapses, rises, or vanishes (TerrainCorruptionManager).
  • Day 6, midnight: the Void Rooms teleport structure appears; time freezes until the control block is solved.
  • Day 7, midnight: the "dead void gate" opens at the center of the world — a chunk is torn out and falls into the void. Falling in triggers a "jump" screen that drops the player into the Dead Void. Placing the Integrion Core on the altar there summons the final Integrion bossfight. Defeating it closes the story, rolls the end-credits screen, and deletes the world.

Origin World

Structures

The abandoned buildings of the origin world — old players' homes and bases. They generate randomly across your own world, not just in one dimension. Each has its own page.

Mechanics

How they appear

Once per day (every 24,000 ticks, sped up or slowed by World Corruption and /fastevents / /slowevents) the mod rolls one random event from a pool of 34 possibilities. Most are harmless ambient glitches, floating water, bedrock sitting on the surface, a door opening on its own, and so on.

The roll only runs while SkinwalkerManager.isAmbientEncounterSuppressed() is false, meaning only at Corruption Level 1 or 2, never at Level 0 or Level 3, and it never runs inside the Purgatory dimension.

One of those 34 outcomes is lorebuild, roughly a 1-in-34 chance each day it can trigger at all. When it lands, the mod picks one of the 8 structures below at random, loads its NBT (the_corrupted_code:lore_build/<name>), and places it on the surface 8–14 blocks from the player, with no rotation or mirroring. The placement is permanent: there is no despawn timer, and nothing removes it afterward.

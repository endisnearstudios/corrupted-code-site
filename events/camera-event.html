Camera Event

Opens the Windows Camera app and leaves a file on the desktop.

The CameraEventManager is extremely rare, roughly a monthly-scale chance, and only runs in unsafe mode. It opens the Windows Camera app on the player's computer and leaves a camera_data.txt file on the desktop.

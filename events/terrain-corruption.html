Terrain Corruption

One random chunk collapses, rises, or vanishes every day.

Active across the Corruption Level 3 countdown, Day 0 through Day 7, the TerrainCorruptionManager picks one random nearby chunk each day and either sinks it, raises it, or makes it vanish outright.

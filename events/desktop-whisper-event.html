Desktop Whisper Event

Opens Notepad or a real terminal and "types" a personal message.

Even rarer than the camera event, the DesktopWhisperEventManager opens either a fake Notepad window or a genuine command-line terminal on the player's computer, and "types" a personalized message into it.

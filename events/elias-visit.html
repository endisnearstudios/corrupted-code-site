Elias_Th3_D3v Visit

A one-time, Caesar-cipher visit from the mod's fictional creator.

At noon on Day 4 of the Corruption Level 3 countdown, a "developer" account, Elias_Th3_D3v, joins the world, sends a series of Caesar-cipher warnings into chat, and leaves. It runs exactly once per world.

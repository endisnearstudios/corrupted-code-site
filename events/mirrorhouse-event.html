The Mirrorhouse Event

A scripted mirrorhouse spawn, separate from the random lorebuild roll.

On the first in-game midnight of Night 3, a mirrorhouse structure is built directly in front of the player. This is a scripted, one-time event, distinct from the random lorebuild roll that can also place a mirrorhouse, among 7 other structures, later on.

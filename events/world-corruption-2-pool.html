World Corruption 2 Pool

A second random pool that layers extra encounters onto Level 2.

Exclusive to Corruption Level 2, the WorldCorruption2Manager gives a daily chance for Boiled One, Null, Nothing Is Alive, Blackhead / 2 / 3, Eye Player, Someone Is Watching or The Broken One to appear outside of their usual triggers.

This is what makes Level 2 feel noticeably more crowded than Level 1, the same entities that normally need a specific condition can now simply show up.
